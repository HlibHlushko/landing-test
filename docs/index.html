Why dogs?

🦮

Loyal companions

Dogs have lived alongside people for over 15,000 years and read our moods better than almost any other animal.

🏃

Built-in fitness coach

Daily walks keep both of you active. Dog owners walk, on average, far more than people without dogs.

❤️

Good for your health

Time with a dog is linked to lower stress, lower blood pressure and less loneliness.

Ready to adopt?

A few things to think about before bringing a dog home:

  • Match the breed’s energy to your lifestyle.
  • Budget for food, vet visits and insurance.
  • Start training and socialising early.
  • Consider adopting from a local shelter.
